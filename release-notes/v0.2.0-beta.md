## Installing

**Windows**
1. Unzip anywhere and run `UncivVR.exe`. Java and Godot are already included in the zip, so there's nothing else to install.
2. For VR, start your headset's OpenXR runtime first: **Meta Quest Link** (set it as the active OpenXR runtime in the
   Meta Quest Link app, Settings > General) or **SteamVR**. Air Link works. With no headset running, the game opens
   in a window and plays with mouse and keyboard.
3. The first launch takes a little longer while it unpacks the game data.

**Quest**
1. Turn on developer mode for your headset (in the Meta Horizon phone app).
2. Install the APK with [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com) (or `adb install`).
3. In the headset, open it from **Library > Unknown sources**. The first start takes a little longer while it unpacks
   the game data.

## What's in this beta

- **A whole game of Unciv on a tabletop in front of you.** Unciv's own rules, AI and turns run underneath:
  Vanilla or Gods & Kings rules, any civ, map size, difficulty and victory type; quick start, new game, save and load.
- **Units:** select, move and attack on the table, with paths, turn counts and expected damage shown as you aim.
  Every Unciv unit action, plus promotions and upgrades. Other players' moves and battles play back on the table
  after your turn. A big **Move** button on the unit card: press it, then point at any tile, including one your own
  units are standing on.
- **Cities:** a city view that does what Unciv's city screen does: worked tiles, buying tiles, the production
  queue, buying and selling, specialists, focus and religion. Settlers show Unciv's suggested city spots.
- **Empire:** the tech tree, policies, diplomacy and trade, religion, espionage, victory progress, and all nine tabs of
  Unciv's empire overview, on boards you can place anywhere around the table. After a game, replay the map's history.
- **Unciv's Civilopedia, tutorials and popups**, Unciv's icons and sound effects. In VR, sounds come from where they
  happen on the table.
- **Music**, chosen the way Unciv chooses it. Unciv itself ships without music, and so does this download:
  **Settings > Sound > Download** fetches Unciv's track, *Thatched Villagers* by Kevin MacLeod (about 10 MB, needs an
  internet connection). You can add your own tracks to the `Music` folder (see below), named the way Unciv names
  them, such as `Babylon - War.mp3`.
- **Arcade buttons on the table rim** for End turn, Idle unit, Civilopedia, Main menu, Recenter and Settings.
  Everything can be done with the right controller alone: point and pull the trigger.
- **Languages:** the game in any of the ~50 languages Unciv is translated into, and this frontend's own menus
  translated into 30 of them, with right-to-left layouts for Arabic and Hebrew.
- **Make it yours:** backgrounds and tables to stand in, 3D or Unciv-style 2D tilesets and unit sets, and panels.
  You can add your own of each without writing code.
- Controllers are shown as the ones you're holding (Quest Touch, Index, Vive...), with optional button labels.

## Your files

| | Windows | Quest |
|---|---|---|
| Saves, settings, your own music, backgrounds, tilesets, panels | `C:\Users\<you>\.unciv-vr` | The app's own storage |
| Logs (attach these to bug reports) | `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Unciv VR\logs` | Not reachable yet: please describe what happened |

On the Quest, saves live in the app's private storage, so **uninstalling the app deletes your saves**. Updating
in place keeps them.

## Known limitations

- **No Unciv mods yet.** Only Unciv's built-in rulesets (Vanilla, Gods & Kings) can be played. Your own backgrounds,
  tables, tilesets, panels, sounds and music already work.
- **No world wrap** and **no multiplayer**.
- **Translations of this frontend's own menus are drafts** awaiting review by native speakers. Corrections are
  very welcome. Unciv's own game text uses Unciv's translations, and how complete those are varies by language.
  Chinese name entry on the VR keyboard uses Latin letters only.
- **Leader voices** and mods' music aren't played yet.
- **Quest:** text is softer than on PC VR, and frame rate and AI turn times on Large and Huge maps haven't been
  measured yet. Small and medium maps are the safe choice for now.
- The Civilopedia has no search yet.

## Reporting bugs
